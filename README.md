### Why this direction

I wanted to put together a single place where factual information about our shoes and our consumer choices is made clear. Building an emotional understanding towards our unknowing choices in the process by breaking down the steps. Doing research for this and the Sustainability project made me realise how hard this seemingly obvious information is to find without going through many different sights and sources.

### Who is this for

This is for anyone that is curious about shoes, our consumer choices, what they do and how we can make them. 

Black background less harsh, easier to use. Gives more interigty and feels less corporate. Makes the illustrations of the highest contrast/draws eyes there rather then background.

Using traditional drawings, scanned in then image traced in Illustrator to great more subtle texture and feel more authentic/'real'. Feels like it re-iterates the point of the webpage more. I've been making sure these work together and side by side, factoring in the text placement while drawing.

The choices add agency to the information the user is reading, adding weight and showing how many steps it takes just to get to the 'use' portion.

## Progress
Back now goes back exactly one step at a time using a history stack. Each time you press Continue or make a choice, the previous state is saved.

It got a bit more difficult when keeping track and editing the paths even if theyre only miniture, I hadn't created enough separation between them and they would often lay out images and text leaving space for image and words not present in this 'choice path'.

i wanted the images to be layer out next to each other as you make choices but not the text as that much text would be overwhelming and take away from the feeling of choice and progression. I left the images as i still want a visual reminder of each step the user goes through to get the where they are so they can better understand the impact through this as well as reading through the text boxes.

The 'sustainable choice' thing starts false then gets set to true when the user takes any of these paths:

"Buy recycled or bio rubber" (reclaimed path) or

"Check labour rights" (from manufacturing) or 

"Continue sustainably" on the Use box (opshop path).

To give a different 'ending' that better encompasses making multi sustainable choices. It also reflects better what happens if earlier on you make sustainable choices rather then right at the end (the op shop ending vs minimising waste and impact ending)
